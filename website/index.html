Pre-payment assurance

Nobody re-reads the contract. We do.

Most strata invoices are approved on trust, not on evidence. A building manager signs off, accounts processes, the payment goes out — and nobody re-reads the contract. That is how rates drift, CPI gets over-applied, and “extras” quietly become permanent.

What we check, every invoice

  • Agreed fee vs billed fee — the base rate in the contract against what actually appears on the invoice.
  • Escalation & CPI clauses — including partial caps. If the contract allows 60% of CPI, full CPI is an overcharge.
  • Inclusions vs extras — many agreements already include consumables, chemicals or call-out labour. We catch them being billed again.
  • Multi-OC allocation — confirming each charge lands on the correct owners corporation (OC1–OC5), not silently on OC1.
  • Rate-card conformance — call-out, after-hours, public-holiday and annual-service rates against the agreed schedule.
  • Authorisation thresholds — work above the contract’s approval limit must carry a quote and written approval.
  • Term & expiry — invoices raised outside a live contract term, or on a rolled-over rate with no signed renewal.
  • Duplicates and out-of-scope items — the same service billed twice, or a scheduled service charged as extraordinary work.

What you get

  • Approve — invoice matches the contract, with the supporting clause cited.
  • Query — a variance detected, with the exact dollar difference and the clause it breaches, ready to send to the supplier.
  • Recover — historical overcharges identified across past invoices so you can raise a credit request.
  • Audit trail — every check recorded against the invoice, so your decisions are defensible at AGM and on audit.
Works with what you already have. Contracts are ingested from your document store — including scanned agreements — and invoices from email or your accounting platform. No supplier has to change how they bill.
